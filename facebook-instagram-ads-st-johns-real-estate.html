Facebook and Instagram Ads for St. John's Real Estate Agents

Tyler York  ·  Jellybean Row Marketing  ·  November 20, 2026

A real estate agent in St. John's is usually trying to start two kinds of conversations. One is with someone who may sell a house. The other is with someone who may buy one. Facebook and Instagram can show that offer while a person scrolls, including someone in St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, or Conception Bay South who is not searching for an agent that day. Ads about housing follow tighter rules than an ad for a shop or a restaurant. You still choose the amount. The click has to open a page or a number where a person can answer.

Call it a housing ad

About ads for housing says an ad for housing promotes or directly links to a housing opportunity or a related service. The examples include sale and rental listings, and property and house hunting services. A hotel stay is not a housing ad. Neither is a tip about owning or buying a home, when the ad is only a tip. A listing, or an offer to help someone buy or sell a house in St. John's, fits the housing description.

If the people you want to reach are in Canada, you select the housing Special Ad Category when you create the campaign. Meta says the ad may be rejected if that category was required and you did not select it. Selecting it does not limit the ad to people already interested in housing. It limits the tools you can use to pick who sees it, so the ad is harder to aim in a way Meta treats as discriminatory. How to choose a Special Ad Category lists the steps: open the campaign in Ads Manager, choose the category, then choose the country.

Age, gender, and the map are limited

For housing ads that reach people in Canada, About audiences for housing, employment and financial ads says some options are limited or unavailable. Age is generally fixed from 18 to 65+. The audience has to include all genders, and you cannot edit that. You can select a country, a region, a town, or a city. You cannot select a postal code, and you cannot exclude a place. A city, an address, or a dropped pin includes a wider radius. Canada is on the list of countries where that radius is 15 miles (25 kilometres). Lookalike audiences are limited or unavailable. Some demographic, behaviour, and interest options are unavailable, and you cannot exclude detailed targeting.

That is why the ad cannot be limited to one neighbourhood in St. John's, or to one set of postal codes in Mount Pearl. Put the town in the ad and on the page. About location selection says Meta uses a mix of signals and cannot guarantee a perfect match, so an impression, a message, or a lead can come from outside the places you selected.

One ad for sellers, one for buyers

Someone who might list a house is not looking at the same thing as someone looking at a house for sale. Run them as separate ads.

The seller ad can speak to a person in St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, or Conception Bay South who is thinking about selling. Say the next step in plain words: call, or use the form on your page. The buyer ad should show one house you can talk about that day, and name the town. When the house is sold, change the photo and the words. Do not leave last month's listing in the ad.

The page has to agree with the ad. A seller click opens a page about selling, with the towns you cover and a way to reach you. A buyer click opens that listing. On a phone, the number or the form should be easy to find. If the ad says Paradise and the page says only downtown St. John's, the person will not know which one to trust.

Set a cap, then read the ad again

You choose the amount. A lifetime budget is the total for the whole run. Meta calls it a hard cap. A daily budget is the average you want to spend each day over a week, Sunday through Saturday. It is not a cap on a single day. About daily budgets says a day, measured to midnight, will not go over 175 percent of the daily budget, and a week ending Saturday will not go over seven times it. The money for the ads goes to Meta. Managing the ads is a separate fee, on our paid ads page.

Before you turn the ads on, check three things. The campaign uses the housing category, and Canada is the country you selected. The places you entered are towns you actually work. The page still matches the house, or the offer, in the ad.

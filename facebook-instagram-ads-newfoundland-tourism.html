Facebook and Instagram Ads for Newfoundland Tourism

Tyler York  ·  Jellybean Row Marketing  ·  November 27, 2026

The 2027 season is still months away. A bed and breakfast in St. John's, a boat tour out of Bay Bulls or Twillingate, a guided hike, and a restaurant or shop that depends on summer visitors can use these months to set Facebook, Instagram, and Google ads for the bookings they want next year. The ad has to point at a date someone can actually book, in a week you will be open. If the boat is tied up for the winter, name the 2027 departures.

Jellybean Row Marketing Inc. is in St. John's. We manage Facebook, Instagram, and Google ads for small businesses in Mount Pearl, Paradise, Conception Bay South, and across Newfoundland and Labrador.

Reach people who are planning a trip

On Facebook and Instagram you choose the places. About location selection says those selections can reach people who live in a country, region, or city, people who have recently spent time there, and people who often visit. Choose St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, or Conception Bay South for a Water Street restaurant or a shop that wants local customers through the winter. For someone in Halifax or Toronto who is planning a first trip for July, add the province or city where that person lives, and write the ad about the 2027 dates. Meta says complete accuracy cannot be guaranteed, so an impression or a message can still come from outside the places you picked.

On Google, the default is Presence or interest. About advanced location options says this reaches people likely to be in, or regularly in, the places you chose, plus people who showed interest through a search, a recent visit, or a page they viewed. Presence keeps the ad for people likely to be located, or regularly located, there. Google says location targeting is a best effort, and that most campaigns see fewer impressions if you switch away from the default. Use Presence or interest when the search is for a St. John's inn, a whale tour, or a Newfoundland hike and the person may be in another province. Presence on Newfoundland stays with people likely to be here. Hotel campaigns use only Presence, so an inn on that campaign type is aimed at people likely to be in the places it targets.

Run the two jobs as two campaigns. One is for people who can visit this week. One is for people booking 2027. A restaurant that wants winter locals and summer visitors should give each offer its own ad. How a St. John's restaurant chooses between Google and Facebook is in our restaurant ads note.

Send them to a page that can take the booking

Use a photo you took of the room, the boat, the trail, or the plate. Name the place. A view of Quidi Vidi, Cape Spear, Bonavista, Trinity, Twillingate, or Gros Morne should be the view the guest will actually get. Say the month in 2027 and what is included. If the picture is from a season you are not running, say which month the photo is from, and which month the booking is for.

Open the ad on the page where that room, tour, or table can be requested. The page has to work on a phone. The dates in the ad, on the page, and on your Google listing should match. How the listing works is in our local SEO note. If the guest finishes on another company's site, keep a clear link on your page to that next step. You can see the click that left. A booking that finishes on the other site may not show up in your ad account.

Show the ad again to people who already looked

Someone who opened the page and left can see the ad again. On Facebook and Instagram, a website custom audience uses the Meta Pixel to reach people who visited your site. About website custom audiences says you can use that audience for people who visited and have not booked, and that Meta may also add people from activity on Facebook and Instagram. On Google, you can create an audience of people who visited your website and show ads to them. The Google tag adds those visitors to the audience, and it can also count a booking or a form completed on your own site. Show the same room or the same departure they already opened. Remove a date once it is full.

Posts for people who already follow you are a separate job. Those are in our social media notes. The money in the ad account goes to Google or Meta. Management is a separate fee, on our page for Facebook, Instagram, and Google ads in St. John's.

Check the places, the dates, and the page

Before you add money, check who the ad is aimed at, the last day it should run, and the page it opens. Pause a departure, a room type, or a week when it is full. If the dates on the site change, change the ad the same day. A winter ad for people in St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, and Conception Bay South can keep running on its own. Stop the 2027 ad when you no longer want more bookings for those weeks.

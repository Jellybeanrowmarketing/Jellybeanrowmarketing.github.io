How social media helps businesses in St. John's

Jellybean Row Marketing  ·  Serving St. John's & all of Newfoundland and Labrador
This is a “how this service helps” guide. We have not invented follower counts, reach, or testimonials.

For a lot of businesses in St. John's and around the overpass, social media is the first place neighbours look. The trouble is time. Posts get skipped, pages go quiet for weeks, and when something is finally shared it may not help a real person decide to call. Our social media management service is meant to keep the page useful and alive.

The typical challenge

You already know you “should post more.” What is harder is knowing what to say, how often to say it, and how to talk with people who comment or send a message — without spending every evening on your phone.

What we do, step by step

  • We learn the business and the people you want to reach in St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, Conception Bay South, or farther afield in Newfoundland.
  • We plan and create posts that explain the work in plain words, not empty slogans.
  • We schedule the work, keep an eye on comments and messages, and report back clearly.

What a business can expect

You can expect a steady presence and content that sounds like a real local business. Social media can help people get to know you, but we will not promise a certain number of followers or sales from posts alone. If this is what you need, see our social media service, the content creation case study, or all case studies.

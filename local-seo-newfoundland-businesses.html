Local SEO for Newfoundland businesses

People in St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, and Conception Bay South already search for the businesses they want to hire. Local SEO is the work of making your business easy to find in those searches, and on the map.

Jellybean Row Marketing handles search engine optimization from St. John's for businesses across Newfoundland and Labrador. This is the same ground we cover with clients. No tricks, and no promised ranking.

Start with your Google listing

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing someone sees. Use the same business name, phone number, and city everywhere. Choose the category that matches what you actually sell. Add recent photos of the work, the place, and the people. Reply to reviews in your own voice.

Give each service its own page

A single homepage that mentions everything is hard to match to a specific search. A page for the service, written in plain language and naming the places you serve, gives Google a clear answer. Link those pages to each other. We do this on our own site for web design, logo design, and Facebook, Instagram, and Google ads.

Make the site easy to use

If the page is slow or awkward on a phone, people leave. A clear page, a real phone number, and a way to ask for a quote do more than a long list of keywords. Technical cleanup, titles, and headings are part of the SEO work described on our SEO page.

Keep it steady

Local visibility comes from showing up consistently: the listing, the site, reviews, and the occasional useful page. It is slower than ads, and it lasts longer. If you want help with the search side, the ads, or the site itself, send a quote request and Tyler will reply.

Why Reviews Drive Real Revenue

For local businesses, your star rating and review count often decide who gets the call — sometimes more than price.

  • More calls: a stronger rating wins customers who are comparing you to competitors.
  • Better local ranking: review signals help lift you in Google's local results.
  • Trust at a glance: consistent, recent reviews reassure people before they ever contact you.
  • Damage control: professional responses keep the occasional bad review from doing real harm.

Reputation Management FAQs

Can you remove bad reviews?

No one can reliably delete genuine reviews, and we won't promise that. What we do is help you earn far more positive reviews and respond professionally, so the occasional negative one carries much less weight.

How do you get more reviews?

With simple systems that prompt happy customers at the right moment and make leaving a review effortless — always within the platforms' rules.

Do you focus on Google reviews?

Google reviews matter most for local businesses, so they're a priority, but we also cover Facebook and other relevant platforms.

Do I get reporting?

Yes — your rating, review volume and trend over time, so you can see the reputation work paying off.

What areas do you cover?

St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, Conception Bay South, and across Newfoundland and Labrador. We are a full-service Newfoundland marketing agency, so reputation work can be paired with SEO and our other services.

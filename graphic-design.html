Graphic Design FAQs

What kinds of things can you design?

Flyers, posters, brochures, menus, signage, social graphics, ad creative, invitations and full event materials — print or digital.

Do you provide print-ready files?

Yes — files are set up correctly for printing, and we can recommend a local printer or work with yours.

How many revisions are included?

We include rounds of revisions so the final design is right; details are confirmed on your quote.

Can you match my brand?

Yes — everything is matched to your logo, colours and style. We also offer logo and business card design.

What areas do you cover?

St. John's, Mount Pearl, Paradise, Conception Bay South, and across Newfoundland and Labrador. We are a full-service Newfoundland marketing agency.
